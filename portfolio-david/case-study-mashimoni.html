Mashimoni School of Hope — Community Library & Youth Empowerment

Location: Mathare, Nairobi Role: Program Coordinator & Community Empowerment Leader, Mashimoni SOH Library

Theory of Change

Goal: To build a gender-inclusive, community-trusted learning environment for underserved youth in Mathare through sustained library stewardship, mentorship, and inclusive programming that addresses practical barriers like menstrual health access.

Intervention Pathway: If we combine consistent library access with youth-led clubs (reading, debate, boys', and girls'), life-skills mentorship, and dignity-focused interventions (monthly sanitary-towel distribution), then young people in one of Nairobi's most underserved communities will build literacy, confidence, and sustained community participation — regardless of gender.

Situation

Mathare, one of Nairobi's largest informal settlements, offered limited safe spaces for structured learning. Young women in particular faced additional barriers to consistent school and library participation tied to menstrual health access and safety.

Task

Supervise the Mashimoni SOH library and lead empowerment clubs for 200+ learners over a five-year tenure — building durable, trusted community infrastructure rather than a time-boxed intervention.

Action
  • Youth-Led Clubs: Led reading, debate, boys', and girls' clubs, building literacy, confidence, and teamwork among youth.
  • Mentorship: Facilitated life skills sessions and career mentorship programmes for holistic youth development.
  • Gender-Inclusive Access: Coordinated monthly sanitary-towel distribution, creating a gender-inclusive, dignified learning space.
  • Community Outreach: Organised community events reaching 900+ adult learners in Kajiado and 1,590+ in Nairobi.

Result
  • Increased youth participation by 28% through targeted community outreach.
  • Sustained a gender-inclusive library and mentorship space over five years of continuous operation.
  • Built the trust and local relationships that later underpinned multi-site programme rollouts, including the standardised Schools of Hope model and the Kajiado Women Empowerment Programme.

Learnings & Sustainability

Community trust built over years, not months, is what makes later scaled programmes — Training-of-Trainers models, EdTech rollouts — land successfully. This foundational tenure is the reason later multi-site work had community buy-in from day one.
