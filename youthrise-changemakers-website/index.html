OUR VISION

A Kenya where every child, regardless of their postcode, grows up knowing they are capable of excellence.

YouthRise ChangeMakers is rooted in Mathare, Nairobi. We create safe, structured and culturally rooted spaces where children learn, adults grow, and young people become confident leaders of their own futures.

Our planned permanent centre will bring learning, technology, creativity, wellbeing and leadership together under one roof—free for the community.
