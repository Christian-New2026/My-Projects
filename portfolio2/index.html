What I Do

Programming built from the ground up, not the top down

A short positioning statement, and the day-to-day work behind the numbers.

I am an Education & Community Development professional with 5+ years of experience designing inclusive, data-driven programmes that empower underserved youth and refugee populations — from digitising community libraries across 30+ sites in Nairobi's informal settlements to classrooms in Kajiado, Mombasa, the Mt. Kenya region, Arusha (Tanzania), and specialised refugee-camp programming in Kakuma and Kalobeyei.

My work sits at the intersection of programme management, monitoring & evaluation, and EdTech integration — translating donor requirements and community needs into delivery plans field teams can run, and turning field data into decisions leadership can act on.

  • 01Design and manage multi-site education programmes reaching 3,500+ learners, including specialised refugee-camp initiatives across 11 sites within the Kakuma refugee complex and Kalobeyei settlement
  • 02Develop grant proposals and secure donor funding, including $5,000+ (KES 650,000) from the Desai Memorial Foundation, while upholding rigorous financial transparency and stakeholder alignment
  • 03Digitise community libraries and integrate EdTech tools across 30+ sites in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda via participatory community workshops
  • 04Implement child safeguarding protocols with 100% compliance across all partnering communities and refugee sites
  • 05Utilise advanced M&E and data frameworks — including UWEZO assessments and real-time progress monitoring — to turn collaborative field data into strategic decisions and course corrections
