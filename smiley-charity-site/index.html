Why we exist

Higher education shouldn't require a building.

Founded by Professor Gunnar Stefánsson of the University of Iceland, Education in a Suitcase began as literally that — tablets and a laptop, carried to a first pilot site. It has since grown into Smiley Charity: a decentralised network of community learning hubs serving students in slums, refugee settlements, and island communities who would otherwise be locked out of university preparation.

Instead of building schools, we equip existing community spaces — libraries, youth centres, church halls — with shared tablets, backup power, and the tutor-web curriculum, so students can study KCPE and KCSE material at their own pace, offline if needed.

The loop

Solve. Earn. Redeem.

The same cycle runs at every hub, whether the nearest city is Nairobi or Reykjavík.

01

Study a drill

Students log in at smileytutor.is with a pre-registered account and work through maths, statistics, or KCPE/KCSE-aligned questions.

→
02

Ace the lecture

Answer enough questions correctly to reach a grade of 9.75, and the lecture is marked aced.

→
03

Earn SmileyCoin

Acing a lecture credits SmileyCoin (SMLY) to the student's tutor-web balance.

→
04

Redeem a device

Coin is sent to a personal SmileyCoin wallet and redeemed for a tablet or laptop once the threshold is reached.
